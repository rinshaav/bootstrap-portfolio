Prime Minister of India

India is a country of immense potential, and I have dedicated my public life to contributing to its development and progress.

About Me

I have served as the Prime Minister of India since 2014. Before becoming Prime Minister, I served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014. I am associated with the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

My focus in public service has included economic development, digital transformation, infrastructure, entrepreneurship, renewable energy, social welfare, and strengthening India's engagement with the international community. I believe in using technology, innovation, and development initiatives to create opportunities and support India's growth.
